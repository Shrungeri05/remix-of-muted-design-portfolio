The Spectator Journey

One river. One ticket.
Door to gate.

Victoria Park sits at the city's heart, and the river reaches it from both directions. Spectators board along the riverfront precincts, converge at the CBD edge, and transfer a short last-mile to Brisbane Stadium. No car. No car park. No new road.
